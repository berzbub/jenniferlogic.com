Golden Harbour Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19. Operated by Jennifer Logic.

Data we handle

Patient names, contact numbers, appointments, inventory and clinical notes entered by staff are stored on the device only. We do not transmit this data to our servers or to third parties.

Microphone and speech recognition

When you tap "Start Voice Input", audio is converted to text using Apple's Speech framework, which may send audio to Apple for processing under Apple's privacy policy. Audio is not stored by the app.

Notifications

The app schedules local notifications on your device to remind staff to call patients. No notification data leaves the device.

Your choices

You can delete records in the app or by deleting the app. You can revoke microphone, speech and notification permissions in iOS Settings.
